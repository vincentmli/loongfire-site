  • The Bug That Broke the Boot: How a glibc Update Silently Crippled dracut 一个让启动失败的 Bug：glibc 更新如何悄然瘫痪了 dracut

    2026-10-27 · LoongFire Team 2026-10-27 · LoongFire 团队

    A single glibc 2.44 change to the fts implementation silently broke dracut's module discovery, causing the LoongFire installer to fail to find its own USB medium. This is the story of bisecting 550 commits, discovering the bug was architecture-independent, and the near-miss for IPFire Core Update 205.

    glibc 2.44 对 fts 实现的一次变更悄然破坏了 dracut 的模块发现，导致 LoongFire 安装程序找不到自己的 U 盘介质。这是一个关于二分查找 550 个提交、发现该 bug 与架构无关，以及 IPFire Core Update 205 惊险躲过一劫的故事。
